Captures can be put on a styled background: gradients, wallpapers, plain colours or your own picture, with padding, shadow, rounded corners and an aspect ratio such as 1:1, 16:9 or 4:5. Works for PNG, MP4 and GIF. Turn on **Recording > Save a styled copy of each capture**, use **Recent Captures > Save Styled Copy**, or run `handset --restyle <file> --style "bg=gradient:dusk;padding=12;radius=6;shadow=60;aspect=16:9"`. The original is always kept.
