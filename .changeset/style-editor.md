A style editor with a live preview: **EDIT** on the toast that appears after a capture is saved (next to **SHOW**), **EDIT** on the toast that appears after a capture is saved (next to **SHOW**), **Recent Captures > Edit Style...** (or `handset --edit <file>`) opens a window to pick the background (gradients, wallpapers, your own picture or a colour), padding, corner radius, shadow and aspect ratio, then exports a styled copy of a PNG, MP4 or GIF. Your last export's look is remembered. Recordings can be played and scrubbed in the editor's preview (Space plays and pauses). The editor has its own icon in the Dock. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes a change, a Loop switch repeats the preview, video exports show their progress and can be cancelled, and a capture dropped on the editor opens in a new window.
