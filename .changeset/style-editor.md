A style editor with a live preview: **EDIT** on the toast that appears after a capture is saved (next to **SHOW**), **EDIT** on the toast that appears after a capture is saved (next to **SHOW**), **Recent Captures > Edit Style...** (or `handset --edit <file>`) opens a window to pick the background (gradients, wallpapers, your own picture or a colour), padding, corner radius, shadow and aspect ratio, then exports a styled copy of a PNG, MP4 or GIF. Your last export's look is remembered. Recordings can be played and scrubbed in the editor's preview (Space plays and pauses).
