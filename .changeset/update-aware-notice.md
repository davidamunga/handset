Update notices know how Handset was installed. With Homebrew, Scoop or winget the toast gives the command to run (`brew upgrade --cask handset`, `scoop update handset`, `winget upgrade davidamunga.Handset`) and a click copies it. The menu gains **What's New in X**, **Skip X** (that version stays quiet) and **Remind Me in 3 Days**.
