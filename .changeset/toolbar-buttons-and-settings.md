The toolbar now has Back, Home, Recents, Power, Volume Up, Volume Down and Notifications buttons, and shows a button's name when you hover it. Menu choices (frame, toolbar, capture format, normalise, clipboard copy and sync, mute) are remembered in `~/.config/handset/settings`.

The window opens smaller on macOS so it fits above the Dock, and can be resized by dragging a corner, or with Cmd/Ctrl plus `+`, `-` and `0` or the Larger, Smaller and Default Size menu items. Scroll-resizing no longer grows past the screen.
