Looks: save the editor's current style under a name and pick it again later from the **Looks** menu at the top of the editor. Five come with Handset (Clean white, Dark card, Sunset 4:5, Aurora 16:9, Dusk square). `--style` also accepts a look's name, for example `handset --restyle shot.png --style "Aurora 16:9"`.
