"Type an address" and "Pair with a code" on the connect screen now open a text field drawn in Handset's window, with Cancel and Connect (or Pair) buttons, instead of a system dialog. They now work on Linux desktops without `zenity` or `kdialog`, such as a stock Omarchy. Type, use Backspace, press Enter to connect or Escape to cancel, and paste with Cmd-V or Ctrl-V; the pairing code field takes six digits.
