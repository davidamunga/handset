Share in a Browser, in the right-click menu, lets someone on the same network watch the phone from a web page, with no app to install. Handset asks you about each person before they see anything: Allow and Control lets them tap, swipe, type and press Back and Home from their browser, Watch Only does not. It shows how many are watching, and Stop Sharing ends it. With audio on, the page has a sound button.
