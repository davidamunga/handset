Handset has a new icon: a phone with two eyes in front of an Android-green and an iOS-blue echo, so it no longer looks like it is only for Android. The connect screen is now grey and white, and the style editor uses a grey look that follows your system's light or dark setting. The Dock icon still shows what Handset is doing, now through the phone's eyes. The editor's **Looks** menu is now a **Presets** card at the top of the sidebar, with a thumbnail of your picture for each preset.
